I keep business websites fast, secure, and online.

Your website, kept working, and answering visitors, around the clock.

For 20 years I have kept business websites fast, secure, and online, WordPress, Drupal, Joomla, and custom database sites, not just designed and left behind. I also build and manage a Website Agent for your site: a help chat that answers visitors from your published pages, day and night, and hands bookings, prices, and decisions to a person. It does not guess, book, change a price, or start work on its own.

I check every change against your live site, backup first, test forms and checkout, and send a plain-English note of what changed. If it breaks, you call me.

Available now for contract engagements (1099), website care plans, and one-time projects.

Sites are built to a clear working standard: clean structure search engines can crawl and understand, code checked for errors, good page speed, and WCAG 2.2 AA held as my working standard. That is a foundation for being found and used, not a promise of top rankings, and not a compliance certificate. What it does promise is a named person who builds it, manages it, and stays accountable for it.
