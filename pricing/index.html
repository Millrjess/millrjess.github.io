How Pricing Works

Most of my prices are flat fees set by scope, not by the hour. A Site Health Audit starts at $500. A Website Standards Pass starts at $750 for a small site.

A strategy session is different: $200 per hour, in one-hour blocks, paid at booking. Ad hoc development work is also available at that rate, when agreed in advance.

For a service with a listed starting price, I quote the exact price after a short review. The price is fixed before work starts.

The Free 15-Minute Discovery Call carries no obligation, and it is the only free service I offer.

Rates and Payment

I am based in North Andover, MA 01845, and serve remote clients nationwide.

Every engagement follows the same standards I hold my own site to: valid W3C code, WCAG 2.2 AA as my working standard, and user experience grounded in published research. Published rates are fixed. I will not cut those standards to lower a price. Scope can be adjusted to fit a budget, with a clear statement of what is included and what is not.

Care plans are billed monthly in advance, on the 1st. Annual payment gives two months at no charge: $1,500 a year for Essential and $3,000 a year for Professional. One-time projects are 50 percent on signing and 50 percent on delivery. If a project payment is not received halfway through the project, work pauses until payment is received. If payment is 14 days past due, new work pauses. If payment is 30 days past due, services are suspended. Work and services resume when the account is brought current. A signed Website Services Agreement is required before work begins.

Month-to-month care plans can be canceled with 30 days' notice. Annual prepay cancellation and refund terms are defined in the proposal before payment. To cancel, complete the Care Plan Cancellation Form (PDF), sign it, and email it to millrjess@gmail.com.

A small budget starts with a smaller standalone scope, not a reduced rate. Options include a Site Health Audit from $500, a strategy session at $200 per hour, or phased work, with critical fixes first and later phases priced separately. If your budget is below the smallest standalone scope, you can still ask about options before you book, with no obligation.

How an Engagement Works

  1. First call: Start with a short phone call to discuss your website, goals, and immediate concerns.
  2. Written proposal: You receive the scope, timeline, price, and deliverables. We sign a Website Services Agreement before work begins.
  3. Work and reporting: I complete the agreed work and provide a written summary of changes, findings, or recommended next steps.

Care plan start dates are confirmed during onboarding. Incident response timing depends on severity, access, and availability; I confirm a start time in the proposal.
