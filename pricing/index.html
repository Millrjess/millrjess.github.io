One-time Services

Which audit do you need? Site Health Audit: you need an overall technical assessment and prioritized next steps. Website Standards Pass: you want scoped markup, accessibility, and usability corrections. Accessibility Audit and Remediation: accessibility is the primary focus and you need a defined accessibility engagement.

  • Site Health Audit, from $500: A review of technical SEO, security configuration, and website performance. You receive a prioritized action plan identifying issues, recommended improvements, and next steps.
  • Independent Usability Studies, $1,000 per round: Independent usability studies coordinated from the beginning, through iteration, to implementation and launch. Each round covers one user group, one moderated round, typically 5 users, and prioritized findings. Pre-launch checks for W3C validation, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, security and malware, speed, broken links and forms, and analytics are included at launch. Additional rounds are $1,000 each. Participant recruitment and incentives, if needed, are quoted separately in the proposal and are not included in the per round price.
  • Front End Development, from $1,500: Responsive interfaces built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Available for new projects, improvements to existing sites, and rescue work. Accessibility requirements and supported browsers are agreed in the project scope.
  • Custom Web App Development, from $5,000: Custom web applications scoped around your business needs. Features may include installation as a progressive web app, offline functionality, Stripe payment processing, dashboards, tracking tools, and accessible interfaces. See New Chapter: GLP-1 Tracker™ as an example of independent product work.
  • Website Agents, from $2,500: Custom website agents for lead qualification, customer support, data processing, and multi-step workflows. Integrations, permitted actions, and human review requirements are defined before development begins.
  • Website Standards Pass, from $750: W3C validation, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility corrections, and user experience fixes based on Nielsen Norman Group research for your existing site or web application. Small sites up to 10 pages from $750; medium sites from 11 to 30 pages from $1,500; large sites of 31 pages and up from $2,500. Web applications and very large sites are quoted individually after a short review.
  • WCAG Accessibility Audit & Remediation, from $750: full accessibility review with prioritized fixes to meet WCAG 2.1 AA and reduce accessibility barriers. The starting price covers a small business site. Larger sites and web applications are quoted individually after a short review.
  • Technical SEO & Search Optimization, from $1,000: Technical improvements that help search engines access and understand your website. Work may include structured data, crawl and indexing fixes, page speed improvements, and GA4 measurement setup. The proposal identifies the issues to address and the deliverables. Search rankings are not guaranteed.
  • Analytics & Reporting Dashboards, from $1,000: GA4 configuration, custom dashboards, and automated reporting built around the questions your business needs to answer. The scope defines the data sources, events, reports, and integrations included.
  • Malware Removal & Incident Response, from $500 per incident: Investigation, malware cleanup, vulnerability remediation, and security hardening within the agreed scope. Urgent requests are prioritized according to severity and availability.

The Accessibility Audit and the Standards Pass are different services. The Audit is a review with prioritized fixes to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, the legal benchmark version. The Standards Pass is a focused review and correction against WCAG 2.2 AA, the practice standard, which encompasses 2.1. The proposal identifies the WCAG version, conformance level, pages, templates, interactions, and testing methods covered for the service you choose.

Rates and Payment

Jessica A. Miller is based in North Andover, MA 01845, serving Boston, Greater Boston's North Shore, and the Merrimack Valley, plus remote clients nationwide. She has 20 years of experience keeping business websites fast, secure, and online.

Every engagement follows the same standards I hold my own site to: valid W3C code, WCAG 2.2 AA held as my working standard, and user experience grounded in published research. Published rates are set. They are not lowered by cutting those standards. Scope can be adjusted to fit a budget, with a clear statement of what is included and what is not.

Care plans are billed monthly in advance, on the 1st of each month, with two months at no charge when paid annually. One-time projects are 50 percent on signing, before work begins, and 50 percent on delivery. Work may be paused after 14 days past due, and services may be suspended after 30 days past due, until brought current. A signed Website Services Agreement is required before work begins.

Month-to-month care plans can be cancelled anytime, with notice as listed in the proposal. Annual prepay cancellation and refund terms are defined in the proposal before payment.

If your budget is small, start with a smaller standalone scope, rather than a reduced rate. Options include a Site Health Audit from $500, a strategy session at $200 per hour, or phased work, with critical fixes first and later phases priced separately. If a budget is below even the smallest standalone scope, the complimentary 15-minute discovery call is the right place to establish that the scope does not yet fit, with no charge and no obligation.
