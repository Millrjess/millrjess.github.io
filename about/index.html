About Jessica A. Miller

Who I Am

Experience AI Can’t Replace

My career has evolved alongside the web. It began with years as a corporate webmaster, continued through the early days of multimedia design, and reaches into today’s AI-accelerated landscape, where seasoned human oversight matters more than ever. When the pandemic hit, I stepped back to focus fully on my daughter. I spent the decade as a full-time mom and novelist while keeping my technical edge through continuous certification, earning 12 professional certifications across cybersecurity, data analytics, and UX design. She just started high school, and I am ready. I am bringing all 23+ years back to client work.

What I Do

AI can generate content, draft code, and build dashboards. It can’t be accountable for your business. I provide the judgment, security expertise, and reliability that only come from two decades of owning production systems.

  • Secure Web Operations: WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla management, hardening, updates, backups, and uptime monitoring, plus custom database-driven sites. Your site stays online and safe while you run your business.
  • Rapid Incident Response: malware removal and critical fixes. When something breaks at the worst moment, you call a person, not a chatbot.
  • Technical SEO and Performance: site health audits, structured data, and page-speed work that keep you visible through every algorithm shift, including AI-driven search.
  • Analytics, Human-Led: GA4, dashboards, and automated reporting, plus a human who tells you what the numbers mean and what to do next.

The Philosophy

I believe that the most effective digital strategies are data-informed but human-led. Whether I am cleaning a complex dataset to find a conversion bottleneck or wireframing a new application, my focus remains on clarity, efficiency, and accessibility.

I do not just implement tools; I build the systems that allow your business to scale in an AI-first world.
