Independent Usability Studies

I coordinate independent usability studies from beginning to launch. Real users test a website before it goes live. I test a site as an outsider, not as its designer, so I am not grading my own work. For websites designed by another provider, I offer an independent usability assessment. Where I have contributed to the design or build, I disclose that involvement and distinguish user testing from an independent review.

Why you need it: launch risk is revenue risk. Real users find problems a designer cannot see. Independent testing before launch, by someone who did not build the site, shows where visitors get stuck, so you fix it before it costs you customers and reputation.

What is included in Independent Usability StudiesRead moreShow less
  • Beginning: goals, user groups, tasks, and success measures are agreed, with participants recruited to match those users.
  • Iteration: small, moderated rounds, typically 5 users per user group per round, with fixes between rounds and a retest after changes
  • Implementation: prioritized findings and specific recommendations for design and development, including a Website Agent where a study shows customers need help online
  • Pre-launch checks: W3C validation, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility testing, security and malware checks, speed testing, checks for broken links and form errors, and analytics verification
  • Taking a website to launch: findings, corrections, and retest results are documented, with remaining issues and decisions needing your input listed.
  • User groups, tasks, rounds, pages, and testing methods covered

See pricing for Independent Usability Studies.
