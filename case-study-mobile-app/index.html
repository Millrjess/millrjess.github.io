Capstone Project: UX Design Mobile App

Transforming Customer Experiences: A UX Design Capstone Project for Foodie Explorer

📥 Download Full Capstone Project (PDF)

Project Overview

Product: Mobile App "Foodie Explorer"
Duration: 3-6 months
Role: Lead UX Designer
Responsibilities: User research, wireframing, prototyping, usability testing, and accessibility improvements

The Problem

David, a busy professional, needs a reliable and efficient way to look up recipes and order groceries online, as his schedule leaves him little time for grocery shopping.

The Goal

Develop the Foodie Explorer app to help users search for recipes and order groceries for delivery, focusing on convenience, meal planning, and support for local businesses.

Understanding the User

Conducted user research through surveys and usability tests to understand the needs of busy professionals. Key insights led to a redesign emphasizing easy-to-use search capabilities, personalized suggestions, and simplified navigation.

User Research: Pain Points

  1. Time Constraints: Users struggle to find time for grocery shopping and meal planning.
  2. Inconsistent Quality: Frustration with previous grocery delivery services.
  3. Complex Navigation: Difficulty navigating existing apps.
  4. Lack of Personalization: Limited tailored recipe recommendations.

Persona: David Miller

  • Age: 42
  • Occupation: Freelance Photographer
  • Goals: Save time, enjoy family time, explore new foods, support local businesses, simplify life
  • Frustrations: Time constraints, grocery store hassle, limited cooking time, finding quality ingredients, inconsistent quality

User Journey Map

David's journey highlights his need for a seamless grocery shopping experience, saving time, and ensuring high-quality products.

Features and Benefits

  • Smart Recipe Discovery: Search recipes based on ingredients, dietary needs, cuisine, or cooking time.
  • Quick & Easy Recipes: Meals prepared in 30 minutes or less.
  • Step-by-Step Visual Guides: Clear instructions and visuals for all skill levels.
  • Integrated Grocery Lists: Automatically create shopping lists.
  • Meal Planning Tools: Organize weekly meals.
  • Community Features: Share recipes and find inspiration.

Value

Recipe Explorer simplifies meal prep, reduces waste, and empowers users to eat better without hassle.

Starting the Design

  • Paper Wireframes: Initial concepts and layouts.
  • Digital Wireframes: Enhanced designs with usability in mind.
  • Low-Fidelity Prototype: Basic prototype to test functionality.

Usability Study: Findings

  1. Intuitive Search: Simplified recipe discovery.
  2. Visual Guides: Increased user confidence and engagement.
  3. Streamlined Navigation: Minimalist design improves user experience.

Refining the Design

  • Mockups: Improved personalization and accessibility.
  • High-Fidelity Prototype: Finalized designs for detailed testing.

Accessibility Considerations

  • High-contrast mode and readable fonts
  • Voice search and screen reader compatibility
  • Clear navigation and large tap targets

Going Forward

Takeaways

The redesigned app streamlines grocery shopping and recipe discovery, enhancing user experience and saving time.

Next Steps

  1. Conduct further usability testing.
  2. Enhance personalization with AI-driven recommendations.
  3. Expand accessibility features for a diverse user base.
