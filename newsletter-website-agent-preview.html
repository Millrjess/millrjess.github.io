AI Agents and Website Operations

Your website can answer, qualify, and book, day or night

Newsletter draft — Website Agents by Jessica A. Miller

Visitors leave when they cannot get an answer. After-hours traffic goes unanswered. Unqualified inquiries take your time. Bookings get missed. Accessibility problems turn visitors away before they ever reach you.

What the agent does

Your website agent answers in the chat, any time. No waiting for office hours. The agent asks the right questions first, so you only talk to people who fit. Meetings book and reschedule straight into your calendar, only on the days and times you choose. Poor fits get a polite no before they reach you.

What it costs, and what it saves

The agent costs $0 a month to run. You own it. Rented alternatives charge forever. Intercom charges $9.99 per qualification, plus a seat subscription. Alenta starts at $39 a month, billed annually. You own nothing with either one.

The agent pays for itself with new clients, and keeps booking after that. The agent works 168 hours a week. A person works 40. A human on call around the clock costs about $180,000 a year in wages alone, about $255,000 with benefits and payroll taxes. The agent costs $5,000 to build and $300 a month to oversee. That is $8,600 in year one and $3,600 a year after.

How it performs

The agent checks the site every hour and reports once a week. The agent watches for accessibility problems, proposes the fix, and puts back what breaks. Nothing changes without your approval. Your report is built from your site and delivered to your email every Monday. I watch the agents, update them, and fix what breaks, so the AI keeps running smoothly.

What it does for your visitors

Visitors can talk instead of typing, and the agent reads aloud. Nothing is recorded. The agent offers help on its own when someone is stuck, at most once per visit. The agent never nags. Returning visitors who opt in are greeted by name. Visitors can erase what the agent remembers with one tap. The agent humanizes the website experience, and that builds customer satisfaction and loyalty.

What the agent never does

The agent never takes payment. The agent never changes a price. The agent never confirms a paid session. The agent never starts work. The agent never promises a date for work. Your rules are followed the same way every time. Nothing is promised that you did not approve. Only you confirm a session or a price.

Proof

Website agents from $2,500. Booking agents from $5,000. I take on a few clients at a time, and I oversee each agent myself. A care plan keeps the agents working properly. Essential $150 a month. Professional $300 a month.

What you get for $5,000: a booking agent that books and reschedules straight into your calendar, only on the days and times you choose.
