Privacy Policy

New Chapter: GLP-1 Tracker™ | Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains what data the New Chapter: GLP-1 Tracker™ app collects, where it lives, and what choices you have. We wrote this policy in plain language because you deserve to understand this policy.

Your tracking data stays on your device

Everything you log in the app (water, protein, movement, injections, weight, measurements, photos, journal entries, side effects, and moods) is stored only in your phone's browser storage. Your logged data never leaves unless you choose to send it, for example to your doctor through your medical portal, or when you download a backup. If you delete the app or clear your browser's website data without exporting a backup first, that data is gone. Use the backup feature in Settings to keep a copy.

I cannot see your entries. No one at New Chapter can.

The AI Wellness Companion

When you send a message to the Companion, your message and a short summary of today go to Cloudflare to get a reply. The summary is not your full log. The summary includes your medication, dose, and shot day; today's protein logged against your target, movement minutes, and water; your latest weight and change since starting; and recent side effects from the last 30 days, up to five. The summary does not include food names, journal text, moods, photos, measurements, or injection times. Your chat messages are not stored on our servers, and they are not used to train AI models. I cannot see your chats. No one at New Chapter can. Your recent chat messages are kept on your device (the last 20) so the conversation has context.

Companion memory (optional, off by default)

Companion Memory is different. Companion Memory is optional, and Companion Memory is off by default. If you turn it on, Companion Memory stores your name, your goals, your preferences, your medication, your dose, and your shot day, so the Companion can personalize its replies. That saved memory is kept until you delete the saved memory with Forget me on the Companion tab. We do not store full conversation transcripts.

Companion Memory is opt in. Companion Memory is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time from the Companion tab. Turning it off deletes your stored profile.

Usage statistics

Usage statistics are collected through Google Analytics, such as which screens are viewed. Your tracked entries, photos, and journal content are not included in analytics events. Google Analytics sets cookies on this site to count visits.

Payment information

Your payment information goes to Google Play or the Apple App Store, depending on where you subscribe. No health data goes to either one.

Purchases and access

When you ask for an access code, we check the email from your purchase against your subscription and issue a 6-digit code, valid for 24 hours. The code is tied to the device that first redeems it. We check your subscription status to unlock the Companion and new entries.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show you ads.
  • We never share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can:

  • Export your data at any time from Settings.
  • Delete all your app data at any time from Settings ("Delete tracking data on this device"). This deletes your tracked entries, backups saved in the app, chat history, and custom activities. Your companion access stays connected.
  • Delete your companion memory at any time from the Companion tab ("Forget me").
  • Ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing millrjess@gmail.com. We will confirm within 30 days.

Security

Data sent to our service is encrypted in transit. Stored profiles are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use industry standard protections.

Children

This app is made for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects your data, we will note it here with a new date and highlight the change in the app.
