Privacy Policy

A New Chapter Tracker — Last updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the A New Chapter Tracker app collects, where it lives, and what choices you have. We wrote it in plain language because you deserve to understand it.

Your tracking data stays on your device

Everything you log in the app — water, protein, movement, injections, weight, measurements, photos, journal entries, side effects, and moods — is stored only in your phone's browser storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app or clear your browser's website data without exporting a backup first, that data is gone. Use the backup feature in Settings to keep a copy.

The AI coach

When you chat with your AI coach, your messages leave your device and are processed by our service provider (Cloudflare) to generate a reply. Messages are not stored after your reply is generated, unless you turn on coach memory (below).

Coach memory (optional, off by default)

If you choose to turn on coach memory, we store a small profile so your coach can personalize its replies. This may include your name, your goals, and preferences you share in chat. We do not store full conversation transcripts.

Coach memory is opt in. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. Turning it off deletes your stored profile.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show you ads.
  • We never share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can:

  • Export your data at any time from Settings.
  • Delete your coach memory at any time from Settings ("Forget me").
  • Ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing privacy@millrjess.github.io. We will confirm within 30 days.

Security

Data sent to our service is encrypted in transit. Stored profiles are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use industry standard protections.

Children

This app is made for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects your data, we will note it here with a new date and highlight the change in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@millrjess.github.io
