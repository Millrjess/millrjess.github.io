Delete my data

Use this page to delete data held by New Chapter: GLP-1 Tracker. Your tracker entries stay on your device, so start in the app.

Delete all your app data

  1. In the app, open Settings and choose "Delete all my data". This deletes your tracked entries, backups saved in the app, chat history, and custom activities. Your companion access stays connected.
  2. To delete your companion memory, open Settings in the app and choose "Forget me". This deletes your stored companion memory profile.
  3. To ask us to delete anything we hold about you, email privacy@millrjess.github.io. We will confirm within 30 days.

If you delete the app or clear your browser's website data without exporting a backup first, that data is gone. Use the backup feature in Settings to keep a copy.
