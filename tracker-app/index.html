About

Medical Disclaimer

This app is for personal tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider about your medication, dosing, and health decisions. Never change your dose based on this app.

Privacy

Stays on this device: everything you track, including water, food, injections, weight, measurements, photos, journal entries, side effects, moods, and your settings. This data is stored in your browser's local storage on this device. It is never sent to our servers, sold, or shared.

AI coach messages: when you chat with your AI coach, your messages leave this device and are processed by our service to generate a reply. Messages are not stored afterward, unless you turn on coach memory.

Coach memory (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in the AI Coach tab, we store your name, goals, and preferences so replies feel personal. You can delete it anytime with the button below.

Privacy Policy

Sent to Google: anonymous usage statistics are collected through Google Analytics, such as which screens are viewed. This helps improve the app. Your tracked entries, photos, and journal content are not included in these statistics.
