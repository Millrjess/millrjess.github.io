What do you do, and what platforms do you work with?

I keep business websites fast, secure, and easy for your visitors to use. A website is only good if people can use it. I work with WordPress, Drupal, Joomla, and custom database-driven websites.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, that’s the core of what I do. Care plans cover updates, backups, uptime monitoring, and support every month. Regular maintenance and monitoring help identify issues early and reduce avoidable disruption. Care plans are available for all the platforms above, not just WordPress.

What is included in a care plan?
  • Essential, $150 per month: CMS updates, daily off-site backups, uptime monitoring, a monthly site health report, and W3C validation.
  • Professional, $300 per month: Everything in Essential, plus security hardening and malware scanning, performance checks, one hour of content or design updates, and priority support.
What if my site has an emergency?

Emergency help is for clients on a care plan. There is no fixed reply window. How fast help arrives depends on the type and severity of the problem. Professional requests are answered before Essential requests during support hours, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern. If you are not on a care plan, incident investigation and cleanup are available as a one-time service, starting with a call and a signed scope of work.

How much do you charge?

Website care plans are $150 per month for Essential and $300 per month for Professional, with two months at no charge when paid annually: $1,500 a year for Essential and $3,000 a year for Professional. One-time services start at $500 for a Site Health Audit. They start at $750 for a Website Standards Pass or a WCAG Accessibility Audit. They start at $1,000 for Technical SEO or Analytics & Reporting Dashboards. Strategy sessions are $200 per hour, with payment at booking. See the full pricing page.

How is the project timeline agreed?

Timing depends on the scope, access, content readiness, outside dependencies, and my current schedule. Your proposal states the scope, timeline, and price before work begins.

Can you work with my existing team or developer?

Yes. I can work alongside your developer, designer, hosting provider, or internal team. We agree who owns each responsibility, how changes are approved, and how issues are handed over. Where needed, I can provide an independent technical review.

Why hire a consultant instead of doing it yourself?

You’re hiring accountability, not just output. I judge whether work is correct and safe before it goes live, and I learn your business well enough to advise you. If your site is simple and you are comfortable maintaining it, doing the work yourself may be appropriate. I am useful when you need someone responsible for coordinating updates, checking affected functionality, documenting changes, and helping you make technical decisions.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. I am accepting website management clients and selected contract engagements. Contact me with your platform, the work you need, and any deadline so I can confirm whether the work is a fit and whether I am available. My About page explains my professional background and career break.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I work remotely from North Andover, MA 01845, serving clients nationwide. Sessions are on Zoom nationwide, or on-site by arrangement in Greater Boston’s North Shore and the Merrimack Valley, including the Andovers.

How do I book a free 15-minute discovery call?

Use the booking page. The 15-minute discovery call is free. A 1-hour strategy session is $200. After payment, I will arrange a time with you, and I will confirm every booking personally before it is final.

Do I pay before we meet?

Yes. For a paid strategy session, you pay before we meet. The session fee is $200, paid at booking through Stripe, with a receipt.

What if we cannot find a time?

The $200 session fee is charged at booking, through Stripe, with a receipt. If we can’t find a time that works, it will be refunded. Refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your card. Once the session has taken place, the fee is not refundable.

What if I cannot attend my session?

If you do not attend a scheduled session, the fee is not refundable. You may reschedule once with at least 24 hours' notice.

How do we start?

Book a free 15-minute discovery call, or contact me. Tell me about your site and the problem you need help with, and I personally read and reply to every inquiry.
