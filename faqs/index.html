FAQs

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. Every engagement is scoped individually, with a clear timeline before work begins. Malware removal is treated as an emergency and prioritized; care plans start immediately.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes — that’s the core of what I do. WordPress care plans cover updates, backups, security monitoring, and support every month, so small issues never become emergencies.

Can you work with my existing team or developer?

Absolutely. I often serve as the technical oversight layer: reviewing work, handling security and infrastructure, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Why hire you instead of just using AI tools?

AI is a tool I use every day — it makes my work faster, not replaceable. What it can’t do is take responsibility: judge whether output is correct and safe, respond when your site is hacked at midnight, or understand your business well enough to advise it. You’re hiring accountability, not just output.

You spent the last decade as a mom and author — are you really taking clients?

Yes. For ten years my full-time job was raising my daughter and writing The Ledger Series novels — and I kept my technical edge the whole way, earning 12 professional certifications between 2024 and 2026 in data analytics, UX design, cybersecurity, and digital marketing. I’m now taking on website management clients and contract engagements, and I bring 23+ years of production experience with me.

How do we start?

Request a consultation — tell me about your site and what’s worrying you. I’ll reply personally — I read and reply to every inquiry.
