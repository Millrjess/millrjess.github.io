Questions, answered.

Clear information before you decide.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo is free, with no sign-in, no credit card, and sample data only. The full tracker with the Companion is $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

How do I cancel, and what about refunds?

Cancel in your Stripe portal (website subscriptions) or in the Play Store (Play subscriptions). Cancel anytime. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you paid for, and you keep access until then. Your first subscription is fully refundable within 30 days on request. After 30 days, no partial refund for the current month or year. Duplicate charges, charges after a confirmed cancellation, and clear payment errors are always refunded in full. Refund requests are answered within one business day.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep your data and lose the Companion. Your tracked entries stay on your device. You can resubscribe anytime.

Is my data private?

Everything you track stays on your phone. I cannot see your entries. No one at New Chapter can. The only thing that leaves is what you choose to send, either to the Companion for a reply or to your doctor through your medical portal. Cloudflare, which runs the Companion, commits to zero data retention and no training on those messages.

Does it give medical advice?

No. It is a personal organization tool and does not diagnose, treat, or replace your prescriber, dietitian, or doctor. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

Is this only for Zepbound?

No. It works with Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic, Mounjaro, and other GLP-1 medications. You enter your own medication and dose.

How do I back up my entries?

Entries save automatically on your device. For a copy of your own, go to Settings, then Backup and restore, and tap Download backup. To restore, tap Restore backup and choose the file.

Can I change the water goal?

Yes, in Settings. Hydration needs vary, so follow any guidance from your healthcare professional.
