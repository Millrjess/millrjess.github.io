    Is this only for Zepbound?

    No. The tracker is designed for people using Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic, Mounjaro, and other GLP-1 medications. You can record your own medication and dose.

    Does the tracker give medical advice?

    No. It is an organization and habit-tracking tool, not medical care. Medication, dose, symptoms, and treatment decisions should always be discussed with a licensed healthcare professional.

    Can I change the water goal?

    Yes. The water goal is adjustable in Settings. Individual hydration needs vary, so follow any guidance your healthcare professional has given you.

    What can I record for exercise?

    You can log daily movement with quick one-tap entries for walks, stretching, and exercise.
