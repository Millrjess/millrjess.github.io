Can I change the water goal?

Yes. The water goal is adjustable in Settings. Individual hydration needs vary, so follow any guidance your healthcare professional has given you.

What can I record for movement?

You can record daily movement in one tap, for walks, stretching, and more.

How do I back up my entries?

Your entries are saved automatically on your device, and the app keeps backup copies. To keep a copy outside the app, open Settings, then Backup and restore, and tap Download backup. The file saves to your phone, in Files under Downloads. To restore, go back to Backup and restore, tap Restore backup, then choose the backup file. A backup is not in Google Drive unless you moved a copy there yourself.

Is my data private?

Everything you track stays on your device. It is never sent, sold, or shared. Your Companion messages are sent only to get a reply. They are not stored, not sold, and not used to train AI models.
