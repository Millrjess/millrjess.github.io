Questions, answered.

Clear information before you decide.

Is this only for Zepbound?

No. The tracker is designed for women using Zepbound and other GLP-1 medications. You can record your own medication and dose.

Does the tracker give medical advice?

No. It is an organization and habit-tracking tool, not medical care. Medication, dose, symptoms, and treatment decisions should always be discussed with a licensed healthcare professional.

Can I change the 80 oz water goal?

The experience centers an 80 oz daily goal. Individual hydration needs vary, so follow any guidance your healthcare professional has given you.

What can I record for exercise?

You can track both strength training and cardio, including the activity and time spent.
