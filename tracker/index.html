    What does a day in the app look like?
    1. Open Today to see your next shot, your water, your protein, and your exercise at a glance.
    2. Log each shot in Injections and keep your dose and site history in one place.
    3. Watch your weight chart move in Weight and mark the milestones along the way.
    4. Close the day in your Journal with a short note about how it felt.
    5. Note any side effects in Effects while they are fresh.
    6. Ask your AI Coach when a question comes up, day or night.
    7. Set your medication, dose, shot day, and goals in Settings.
