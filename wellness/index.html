A New Chapter in My Health Journey

I am documenting my own metabolic health journey in my 50s, and sharing what I learn and what I love along the way.

My story

I recently began a new chapter focused on metabolic health, sustainable weight management, and healthy aging. After years of putting everyone else first, I decided it was my turn. I am learning as I go, asking better questions, and building habits I can actually keep.

I am on Zepbound, and here is the honest truth. When you lose weight fast, your body does not just burn fat. It eats muscle too. After 50, muscle is everything. It is what keeps you strong, steady on your feet, and burning calories at rest. Losing it was not an option for me, so protein became my number one focus.

I picked Zepbound over the other GLP-1s because it works on two appetite hormones instead of one, and the research showed people lost more weight on it. That was my decision with my doctor, and I would tell anyone to have that conversation with theirs.

The medicine curbs my appetite, but it does not feed me. That is on me. I eat the things on this page because I am trying to make sure what I do eat is actually healthy and keeps my muscle where it belongs.

Everything I share here is personal and educational. This is my personal journey, not medical advice. Talk to your own clinician about what is right for you.
