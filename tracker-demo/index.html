    About

    New Chapter: GLP-1 Tracker™ tracks the habits that protect muscle while the medicine works: protein, water, and movement, plus your injections and your weight.

    A GLP-1 lifestyle tool with a personal AI Wellness Companion. Everything in one place.

    Developed by Jessica A. Miller, Founder, New Chapter.

    More than weight loss. A healthier way forward.

    Who it is for: For adults navigating GLP-1 treatment.

    Why it is different: Not another calorie-counting or food-diary app. No obsessing over food. No food guilt. Just practical tools to help you stay informed, build healthy habits, and stay strong.

    Why you need it: Appetite suppression can make it easy to undereat, lose muscle, and become dehydrated. The goal is to lose body fat while supporting your bones, heart, brain, skin, digestive health, and overall nutritional status. Think whole-body nutrition, not just weight loss. The tracker supports these areas through the habits it tracks, protein, water, and movement, plus injections, weight, and side effects.

    Whole-body support: Protein and muscle, bones and strength, heart and brain, skin and digestion, hydration and energy.

    Track medication, injections, weight, protein, hydration, movement, and side effects. Get support from an AI Wellness Companion that can remember your preferences and goals.

    Medical Disclaimer

    This app is for personal tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider about your medication, dosing, and health decisions. Never change your dose based on this app.

    Privacy

    Stays on this device: everything you track, including water, protein, injections, weight, measurements, photos, journal entries, side effects, moods, and your settings. This data is stored in your browser's local storage on this device. It is never sent to our servers, sold, or shared.

    AI Wellness Companion messages: when you chat with your AI Wellness Companion, your messages leave this device and are processed by our service to generate a reply. Messages are not stored on our servers afterward, unless you turn on Companion Memory. Your recent chat messages are kept on this device (the last 20) so the conversation has context.

    Companion memory (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in the Companion tab, we store your name, goals, and preferences so replies feel personal. You can delete it anytime with the button below.

    Privacy Policy

    Usage statistics: not collected in the demo. This demo does not use Google Analytics, so no usage statistics are sent to Google. Your tracked entries, photos, and journal content stay on this device.
