How an Engagement Works

Every engagement starts with a conversation, and every engagement is scoped individually before work begins.

  1. Start with a complimentary 15 minute discovery call to talk through your website and your goals.
  2. You receive a written proposal with the scope, timeline, and price, and we sign a Website Services Agreement before work begins.
  3. I do the work and you receive a written summary or a prioritized action plan.
  4. Care plans start immediately, and malware removal is treated as an emergency.
